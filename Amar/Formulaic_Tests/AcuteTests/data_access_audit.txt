Completed locally: aggregate-level formulas were applied to real published group-mean N2/P2 signals from three NRS bands (assigned midpoint labels 5, 7, 9); leave-one-band-out checks, monotonicity, exact rank permutation, cross-study power-law transfer check, score dispersion and pairwise standardized descriptive differences were computed. Outputs live in aggregate_formula_results/.

Still not executed: subject-grouped CV on real trials; per-trial prediction and pain-level categorization; real-patient subject scores; individual-level MAE/RMSE, calibration, ordinal metrics, cluster bootstrap, participant-preserving label permutation, temporal holdout, feature ablation and cross-dataset replication.
