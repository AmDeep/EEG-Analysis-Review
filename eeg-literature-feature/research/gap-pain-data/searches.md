# Targeted search provenance

Four webSearch queries were run, each with two short variants and count=10:
1. “Which distinct original research papers have reanalyzed the publicly downloadable Zolezzi chronic neuropathic pain EEG dataset using additional pain-related EEG features beyond Ryu 2024, and what datasets/results did they report?” Variants: “Zolezzi Mendeley EEG reanalysis pain”; “neuropathic pain EEG Mendeley reanalysis”.
2. “Which original pain EEG studies or reanalyses use publicly accessible raw laser-evoked EEG cohorts from Ploner OSF, Hu or Zhao and report pain ratings with signal strength or variance features?” Variants: “Ploner OSF pain EEG reanalysis”; “Hu Zhao laser evoked EEG pain data”.
3. “What primary source and public OSF API file-list evidence determines whether Han et al 2026 EEG data are truly publicly downloadable, and are Bott 2025 pooled chronic pain EEG datasets public?” Variants: “Han 2026 pain EEG OSF files”; “Bott 2025 chronic pain EEG public datasets”.
4. “Does Mari et al OSF uqt9z contain public raw EEG files, and what independent papers reanalyze Zolezzi et al 2023 public chronic neuropathic pain EEG dataset?” Variants: “OSF uqt9z Mari EEG files”; “yj52xrfgtz Zolezzi EEG reanalysis”.

Primary pages and repository/API listings were fetched and summarized in adjacent evidence files. Search discovery alone was not treated as proof of full text, raw EEG, or public data.
