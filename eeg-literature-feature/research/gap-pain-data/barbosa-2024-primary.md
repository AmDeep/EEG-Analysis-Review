# Barbosa et al. 2024 secondary analysis of public Zolezzi EEG

Primary full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11309019/
DOI 10.4103/bnm.bnm_17_24. Published in Brain Network Modulation 3(2), 52–60, June 27 2024. Exact title: “Resting-state electroencephalography delta and theta bands as compensatory oscillations in chronic neuropathic pain: a secondary data analysis.” Lead author Sara Pinto Barbosa; coauthors Ygor Nascimento Junqueira, Milena Apetito Akamatsu, Lucas Murrins Marques, Adriano Teixeira, Matheus Lobo, Mohamed H Mahmoud, Walid E Omer, Kevin Pacheco-Barrios, Felipe Fregni.

The article explicitly says it uses data from Zolezzi et al. (2023), with 36 chronic neuropathic pain participants and BPI ratings. Eyes-closed resting EEG was analyzed using relative band power, FFT and five-second windows with 50% overlap; band definitions cover delta through beta, and regional central/frontal/parietal features. It reports no significant multivariate model for pain during EEG or most interference outcomes; the model for average pain intensity in the previous four weeks and a separate mood-interference outcome were significant. Authors describe central alpha associated with higher pain and central delta with lower pain. Do not treat this as held-out prediction performance: this is cross-sectional analysis with outcome screening/regression and no independent test cohort or external validation. Values in model tables are not copied here as validation metrics.

In Ethical aspects, authors state the used data were provided by original authors and “available in a publicly accessible database”; the linked Zolezzi data paper/Mendeley files independently confirm public raw EEG and questionnaires. This is an additional paper-level reanalysis of the same 36 participants, not a new cohort or extra dataset.
