# 2021 Zolezzi proceedings citation is distinct from Ryu 2024 PAC reanalysis

Ryu et al. 2024 primary full text: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-71219-3, DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-71219-3. Ryu identifies the source public cohort as Zolezzi et al. and cites a distinct 2021 proceedings paper titled “Identification of Neuropathic Pain Severity based on Linear and Non-Linear EEG Features.” Crossref metadata for DOI 10.1109/embc46164.2021.9630101 identifies this as a 2021 IEEE EMBC proceedings article, pages 169–173, by Daniela M. Zolezzi, Luz Maria Alonso-Valerdi, Norberto E. Naal-Ruiz, and David I. Ibarra-Zarate.

This establishes that a separate 2021 conference paper exists and must not be overwritten with Ryu et al. (2024), a separately published frontal PAC/asymmetry analysis (2024 DOI above). The 2021 record is not itself evidence for Ryu's later PAC results; the cited title concerns linear/nonlinear EEG features. In this pass, open full-text access to the 2021 proceedings itself was not verified, so it is listed as a checked nonqualifying candidate rather than a new qualifying row. The 2023 journal feature paper and 2024 Barbosa/Ryu analyses also reuse the same 36-person Zolezzi cohort; do not sum them as independent datasets.
