Independent EEG feature test: physical stimulus intensity

Partial result—not complete cross-dataset validation. Great cross-dataset accuracy is NOT validated. Only one cohort had usable dose-labeled poststimulus EEG; the independent second-cohort stage is blocked. This study does not establish prediction of perceived or clinical pain.

2,520 real trials;10 held-out participants;21 candidate families;507 full-model predictors. Target:42,46 or48°C. Four-fold participant-held-out splits, training-only imputation/scaling/CSP, no PCA and no test-set tuning. The original feature-source cohorts were excluded.

Limits on interpretation

  • One independent cohort is scored; multiple participants are not multiple datasets.
  • VR deposited cue epochs end about100ms after shock, too short for the prespecified features; no fake transfer result is supplied.
  • Only10 people; no globally identifying linkage to prove every historical cohort disjointness.
  • Cue expectations, arousal and peripheral artifacts can explain prediction; no causal/pain-specificity claim.
  • MAT voltage units use the stated BrainAmp microvolt assumption; short spectra limit peak-frequency estimation.
  • 21 family ablations and20 permutation controls are exploratory, not independently validated selection.
