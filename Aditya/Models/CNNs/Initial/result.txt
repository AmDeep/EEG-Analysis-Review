 Implemented a compact EEGNet-inspired convolutional regressor for single-trial laser-intensity prediction from 28-channel EEG epochs. The model takes inputs of shape N × 1 × 28 × 250, uses a temporal convolution followed by depthwise spatial filtering and a separable temporal block, and outputs a scalar laser-power estimate for each epoch. The architecture includes 3 main convolutional stages with batch normalization, ELU activations, average pooling, and dropout (0.5), followed by a final linear regression head; the full model has 1,409 trainable parameters. Training was performed with subject-grouped 5-fold cross-validation to prevent leakage across subjects, channel-wise normalization was fit only on training data, and optimization used AdamW with L1 loss and ReduceLROnPlateau scheduling. The final clinical evaluation used a fixed tolerance margin of ±0.5 in laser-power units, yielding an OOF within-margin accuracy of 47.86%, MAE of 0.617, and RMSE of 0.764 across 124 subjects.
